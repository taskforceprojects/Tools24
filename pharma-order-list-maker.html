Guide

Everything you need to know

What is a Pharma Order Sheet Maker?

A free online tool for pharmacies, clinics and distributors in India. Enter your pharmacy details, add medicines with quantity, rate, discount and GST, and get a clean, print-ready purchase order with correct tax calculations in Indian Rupees.

How to create a medicine purchase order?

  1. Enter pharmacy name, GSTIN and drug licence details.
  2. Add medicines or import a CSV / Excel list.
  3. Pick GST, IGST or No Tax mode.
  4. Choose a template, then download PDF, print or save a draft.

Can I download a pharma order as PDF?

Yes — a genuine multi-page A4 PDF with your logo, company block, itemised medicine table, CGST/SGST or IGST breakup, amount in words, notes and signature area. Table headers repeat on every page.

Can I import medicine data from CSV?

Yes. Upload CSV, XLSX or JSON with headers like Medicine Name, Qty, Rate, Discount, GST — columns are auto-detected and parsed entirely in your browser.

Is my order data stored online?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser and drafts are saved to your own device's local storage.
