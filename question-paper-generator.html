Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Question Paper Generator.

What is the Question Paper Generator?

It is a professional academic tool built for teachers, professors, and coaching institutes to design, organize, and export fully formatted exam question papers and answer keys without hassle.

Can I create Hindi or bilingual question papers?

Yes! You can select Hindi or Bilingual (English + Hindi) mode. All Hindi text renders cleanly using Unicode-safe fonts and exports properly in PDFs.

How does automatic mark calculation work?

The tool automatically tallies marks assigned to all questions. If the total differs from your specified Maximum Marks, a warning notification appears with a [Fix / Adjust] helper.

Can I generate Set A, B, C and D?

Yes! You can choose sets from the Paper Details and optionally shuffle questions and MCQ options while preserving total marks and section structures.

Is my paper data stored online?

No. All your paper details, questions, and drafts are processed locally in your browser using secure localStorage. Your academic data remains completely private.
