What is GST?

Goods and Services Tax (GST) is an indirect tax levied on the supply of goods and services in India, replacing multiple earlier taxes.

CGST & SGST

For intra-state transactions, GST is split equally into CGST (Central) and SGST (State) components — each being half of the total GST.

IGST

For inter-state transactions, the full GST amount is collected as Integrated GST (IGST) by the Central Government.

GST Rate Slabs

India has 5 main GST slabs: 0%, 5%, 12%, 18% and 28%. Some items also attract special rates or cess depending on category.
